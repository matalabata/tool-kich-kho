LEMON3 RPA — mang sang may co DIGINET

1) Copy ca thu muc lemon3-rpa sang may kia (USB / OneDrive).
   Khong can copy thu muc .venv (may kia se tao lai).

2) Tren may DIGINET, cai Python 3.12 neu chua co:
   https://www.python.org/downloads/
   Khi cai, BAT BUOC tick "Add python.exe to PATH".

   Neu gap loi "uv trampoline failed" / "entity not found":
   - Xoa thu muc .venv trong lemon3-rpa (neu co)
   - Chay lai run.bat
   Khong copy .venv tu may khac.

   Lan dau cai thu vien: de cua so run.bat mo 3-5 phut.
   Dong "Installing build dependencies" la pip dang build PyAutoGUI, khong phai mat mang.
   Neu cua so bao "Can internet" roi tat som: chay lai, dung tat giua chung.

3) Mo DIGINET, dang nhap, vao:
   Ban hang -> Danh sach hoa don ban hang (D05F9300)
   De nguyen man hinh do len truoc, dung bam F5.

4) Dong file Excel danh sach phieu (neu dang mo). Tool khong ghi de file goc;
   ket qua nam trong artifacts/runs/<thoi-gian>/.

5) Double-click run.bat trong thu muc lemon3-rpa.
   Lan dau se cai thu vien, roi mo cua so Lemon3 RPA.

6) Trong app:
   - File Excel: chon file cot "SỐ PHIẾU"
   - Kich ban: scenarios\xuat_kho.yaml (mac dinh)
   - Tieu de cua so: DIGINET Desktop (khong can D05F9300; do la tab ben trong)
   - Cot so phieu: D  hoac  SỐ PHIẾU
   - Lan dau: bam "Kiem tra tim phieu" (nhap + xac minh luoi, KHONG Ctrl+K, KHONG Luu)
   - "Chi in mo ta" chi in buoc ra man hinh, khong bam DIGINET — khong dung de test loc phieu
   - Lan that: 1 dong phieu trong Excel, bam Chay
   - Dung khan cap: dua chuot vao goc tren-trai man hinh

Luong tool:
  Go so phieu -> doi luoi khop dung ma -> Ctrl+K xuat kho
  -> Chon kho 1000 -> xac minh MNNKXB01 + Dien giai -> Luu
  -> chi SUCCESS khi popup dung "Dữ liệu đã được lưu thành công."

Neu khong nhap duoc o So phieu:
  Click vao o loc duoi cot So phieu, hoac bam "Ghi toa do (3s)".
  Toa do duoc ghi vao ca YAML va giao dien.

Moi lan chay tao thu muc:
  artifacts/runs/<id>/run.log
  artifacts/runs/<id>/screenshots/
  artifacts/runs/<id>/checkpoints.json
  artifacts/runs/<id>/result.xlsx  (ban sao, khong ghi de Excel goc)
